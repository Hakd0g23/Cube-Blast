How to play

Drag blocks from the tray onto the grid. Fill a full row or column to clear it. Press P or Esc to pause.

  • Drag a piece from the tray (bottom row) onto the grid.
  • Filling a full row or column clears it and scatters 1–3 colored "shards" back onto the board — shard color matches the piece that triggered the clear.
  • Shards queue up in the top row of slots. If the queue fills up, the next shard skips the line and force-inserts straight into your tray instead.
  • Difficulty ramps in waves the longer you survive — later waves deal trickier pieces. Current wave shows next to your score.
  • No timer. Game over when nothing left in the tray fits anywhere on the board.
